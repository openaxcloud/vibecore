# Acceptable Use Policy

VibeCore workspaces may not be used for:

- crypto mining
- malware distribution
- credential theft
- phishing
- spam
- port scanning without authorization
- denial-of-service activity
- reverse shells against unauthorized systems
- attempts to access cloud metadata services
- attempts to access VibeCore internal infrastructure
- hosting illegal or abusive content

VibeCore may throttle, stop workspaces, suspend organizations, preserve evidence, or require manual review when abuse is detected.

Public previews are separate from editor access. Sharing a preview never grants access to source code, secrets, terminals, or workspace controls.
